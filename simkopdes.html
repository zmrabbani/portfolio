Simkopdes (Sistem Manajemen KDKMP)

Centralized digital platform designed to streamline administration, financial operations, and membership management for village-level cooperatives.

2025-Present

Visit Platform Details

Simkopdes (Sistem Informasi Manajemen Koperasi Desa/Kelurahan Merah Putih) is an integrated centralized digital platform developed by the Ministry of Cooperatives of the Republic of Indonesia (Kementerian Koperasi RI) under the Assistant Deputy for Digitalization. This strategic initiative is designed to modernize and empower Indonesia's cooperative ecosystem, particularly village-level cooperatives (Koperasi Desa/Kelurahan Merah Putih - KDKMP), providing them with scalable digital infrastructure to manage institutional governance, member registry, and multi-unit business activities.

The platform serves as the digital backbone connecting village cooperatives with government policy makers, enabling real-time monitoring, financial accountability, and transparent management of local economic initiatives ranging from grocery stores and savings-and-loan services to digital product distribution.

In this project, I serve as the Frontend Developer, responsible for architecting and delivering responsive, accessible, and high-performance web interfaces for the Simkopdes web platform. My key responsibilities and contributions include:

  • Frontend Interface Development: Building clean, responsive, and intuitive web interfaces using modern web standards to ensure ease of use for cooperative managers across diverse regions.
  • API Integration: Integrating complex RESTful APIs with frontend components to enable real-time data synchronization, dynamic transaction processing, and automated reporting workflows.
  • Departmental Administration & System Modernization: Supporting the Assistant Deputy for Digitalization in executing strategic directives from ministry leadership to enhance the digital capabilities and operational efficiency of cooperative networks nationwide.
  • Performance & User Experience: Optimizing layout rendering, responsive navigation, and form validations to maintain seamless usability across desktop and mobile devices.

Key Platform Capabilities

  • Integrated Membership & Governance: Digital recording and administration of verified cooperative members with real-time identity validation.
  • Financial & Transactional Operations: Standardized digital ledger, cash flow recording, and financial health overview.
  • Digital Profile & Microsite: Public-facing digital presence for each KDKMP to showcase local products, business units, and legal certifications.
  • Policy & Monitoring Dashboard: Comprehensive executive dashboard for ministries and regional departments to monitor KDKMP progress, growth, and socio-economic impact.
